Code sample, KernelAscent

Repository https://github.com/ahmd-mohsin/KernelAscent

The most relevant files are kernelascent/v3/lab_compounding.py (weight-RSI, three-arm), kernelascent/v3/lab_selfplay_rsi.py (self-play, three-arm), kernelascent/agent_bench.py (grader, roofline, scoring), scripts/consistency_audit.py with tests/test_audit_gates.py (claim gates and their negative tests), and BENCHMARK_LOG.md (the full experimental record). The work is roughly 22k lines of my own Python across 28 experiment labs, 55 scripts, and a gate suite, plus the cluster tooling that runs them.

KernelAscent makes recursive self-improvement falsifiable in code, and the code is organised around the recurrence that defines it. A learner maps weights and self-generated data to new weights, θ(t+1) = T(θ(t), D(t)), where D(t) is drawn from the model's own policy π(θ(t)). Compounding is a claim about the derivative of that map. It says this round's gain grows with the capability already reached, ∂C(t+1)/∂C(t) > 0, and it is confoundable with a model that simply samples more. My three-arm design is a finite-difference decomposition of T built to break that confound. The lineage arm computes T(θ(t), D(t)) and keeps the full recursion. The reset arm computes T(θ(0), D(t)) on byte-identical data, which removes the weight channel and holds data quality fixed. The best-of-N arm never trains and samples θ(0) at the cumulative budget k(r+1). Lineage minus reset isolates accumulation. Lineage minus best-of-N isolates training from search at equal samples. The self-play lab measures an interaction rather than a main effect, because solver weights and task distribution both move. STATIC fixes the frontier, FROZEN-AUTHOR escalates it with a frozen author, and LIVE-AUTHOR escalates it with the evolving model, so L minus S gives the total curriculum effect, F minus S gives the effect with no author update, and L minus F gives the cross term between author quality and solver quality. That cross term is the self-referential signal. Scoring makes heterogeneous tasks commensurable through a per-task affine map anchored on physics, score = 0.5 + 0.5·clamp((s−1)/(c−1)), where s is speedup over the compiled baseline and c is the roofline ceiling t_compiled/t_roofline with t_roofline = max(FLOPs/peak, bytes/bandwidth). The score reports the fraction of physically achievable headroom captured, so a task with 1.1x of slack and a task with 40x of slack weigh the same in the mean.

I am most proud of what the instrument work uncovered, because both findings came from interrogating the measurement instead of the models. The first is a statement about an estimator. The headroom score takes a maximum over k candidates, so if p is the probability that one candidate verifies, the observable tracks 1 − (1−p)^k. Its derivative with respect to p collapses as p grows. At k = 10 a model moving from p = 0.1 to p = 0.9 changes that quantity by almost nothing, and on this bank the score pins at 0.50 the moment one candidate reaches parity. Self-training acts on p. My estimator was blind to the one parameter the hypothesis predicts. I re-scored the identical runs under pass-rate, p̂ = n_ok/k, which is unbiased for p and keeps its sensitivity across the whole range, and registered the change as post-hoc in a pre-registration amendment with its falsifier fixed first. The flat null became monotone compounding. It beat the reset learner, beat matched-budget search by +0.94, and transferred to a held-out task family. One design, one seed, one variable changed, opposite conclusions. The second finding is about state. These runs chunk across cluster walltimes, so θ(t) crossing a chunk boundary is the recurrence itself. My resume restored history and the round counter but not the LoRA adapter, so every resumed chunk silently recomputed T(θ(0), D(t)) while the round index kept climbing. Nothing errored and the queue looked healthy. trainC collapsed at the round named by resumed_at in all 18 resumed cells. The repo now treats defence as first-class. It runs 34 audit gates over cross-referenced claims and a meta-gate that fails the suite when a gate lacks a negative test. It stamps provenance into every artifact, including whether Triton could build. It writes atomically by tmp-then-rename, exits 3 and refuses a resume over continuing on base weights, and keeps a ledger that separates a cell that advances from one that repeats.

Four parts of the code I would build differently, and the first is the optimisation itself. My loop maximises verified reward on self-generated data with nothing holding θ(t) near θ(0), so the step size each round is whatever the data happens to produce. I would make it a regularized RSI objective, θ(t+1) = argmax E[R] − λ·KL(π(θ) ‖ π(θ(0))), and sweep λ. My mechanism probe already logs per-depth LoRA drift and retention on already-solved tasks, and it finds sustained drift and retention discriminate the models that compound. A trust-region term acts on exactly those two quantities, so λ converts drift magnitude and forgetting from observed outcomes into a dial I set, and it gives the recurrence a contraction parameter instead of an empirical one. Second, configuration. KA_SCORE, KA_EXTRACT and KA_MAX_NEW are read from os.environ at import time in separate modules. Two arms of one comparison generated at 2048 and 900 tokens because of it, and two rungs of the ladder still run at 2048 and 1200. I would construct one frozen config object at entry, validate it, stamp it into the artifact, and diff it across runs, which makes incomparability a type error. Third, each lab hand-rolls its own resume and three of four got it wrong in different ways. One dropped the adapter, one wrote non-atomically, one logged a failed restore and continued on base weights. I would have each lab declare its cross-round state once and let shared machinery own the write, the restore and the refusal. Fourth, the metric lives as a branch inside eval_tasks keyed on a string, which couples scoring to generation and blocks re-scoring a finished run. I would persist per-candidate verdicts and compute every metric from the artifact, which turns the best-of-k blindness into an afternoon of analysis rather than a month of compute.
