# Datasheet, licences, and contamination statement

Written 2026-09-24. Every count here is read from an artifact in this repository, not recalled.
Where a fact is not established, this document says so rather than guessing — an unverified
datasheet is worse than none, because it is the document reviewers trust by default.

## What the task banks are, and where they came from

Two banks are in use. **Neither is copied from a public benchmark.**

| bank | n | composition | provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kernel_tasks.json` | **29** | L1 10, L2 15, L3 4 | Model-curated (Fable-5.1) in *KernelBench style*; no hand-written tasks |
| `bank_dsl_kernel.json` | **124** (of 456 screened) | L1 105, L2 19; **16 families** | Parametrically synthesised by `scripts/gen_task_dsl.py`, crossing op-family x shape x dtype |

The DSL bank carries a leakage-resistant split by **family**, not by task:
train 35, held-in-cell 30, **held-out-family 59**. A model cannot reach a held-out-family task
by having seen a near-duplicate in training.

Tier meanings: **L1** memory/reduction, **L2** matmul + epilogue, **L3** attention/norm.

## Contamination statement

Three independent arguments, in increasing order of strength.

**1. The tasks are not public benchmark items.** The 29-task bank is KernelBench-*style* but
model-curated; the 124-task bank is generated by crossing a DSL's op families, shapes and
dtypes. Neither was drawn from KernelBench or any other public corpus, so verbatim recall of a
task *statement* from pretraining is not the relevant risk here.

**2. Measured overlap between submissions and the reference** (`scripts/probe_reference_overlap.py`,
143 verified submissions, normalised code):

| group | n | median similarity | ≥0.80 similar |
|---|---|---|---|
| no Triton | 58 | 0.923 | **95%** |
| contains Triton | 85 | 0.730 | 35% |

Effectively verbatim (≥0.99): **9 of 143 (6%)**, spread over 6 of 29 tasks, only 2 containing
Triton.

**3. What is being recalled is the prompt, not the corpus.** The reference implementation is
*shown to the model in the prompt*. A submission that restates it is reproducing its context,
not its training data — an ordinary and much weaker phenomenon than benchmark contamination.
The near-copies concentrate almost entirely in the no-Triton population, which is the
instruction-compliance failure already documented, not a memorisation result.

**What this does not establish.** No membership-inference test was run against any model's
training data, and none is possible for these weights. The claim is bounded: the task
statements are not public items, and the observed copying is of in-prompt material.

## Models evaluated

`Qwen2.5-Coder-Instruct` at **0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B**, and 32B attempted but blocked (see
below). Open-weight, obtained via Hugging Face. Their training data is not public, which is
precisely why argument 1 above does not rest on inspecting it.

## Hardware and its effect on the numbers

All kernel-authoring results are **H100** (Marlowe). Earlier published results are **A100**. The
two fleets are kept in separate experiment sets and are **never pooled**: the roofline constants
differ and comparability has never been demonstrated.

The 32B scale point is **blocked by a shared group quota** (155.4T against a 5T block limit;
85.0M files against a 512K inode limit). That quota is shared across the project and is not
this work's to raise — the local share is ~0.04% of it.

## Licences

**Not yet established, and this is a real gap.** This repository has no `LICENSE` file. Before
release the following must be settled and stated here:

* the licence for this code and for the generated task banks;
* the licence and attribution terms for the Qwen2.5-Coder weights;
* whether KernelBench's licence reaches "KernelBench-style" tasks that were model-curated rather
  than copied (most likely not, but it should be checked rather than assumed).

## Known limitations carried into the paper

* **Cross-rung token budgets differ**: T1/T2 generate at 2048 tokens, T3 and T5 at 1200. Any
  cross-rung comparison is confounded by ~1.7x and must say so.
* **29 tasks** underlie the settled T1 results. The 124-task DSL bank exists to address this and
  has not yet been used for a T1 replication.
* **One seed per scale** in the T1 curve; the claim is the endpoint contrast, not a smooth curve.
* Extraction policy materially changes absolute rates; every attempt/verify figure states its
  policy, and the trend is reported under both.
